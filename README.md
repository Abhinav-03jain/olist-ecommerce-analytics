# 📊 Olist E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing Brazilian e-commerce data using Microsoft Power BI. This project focuses on understanding business performance through interactive dashboards covering sales, logistics, customer behavior, product performance, and business trends.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of data across orders, customers, products, payments, and logistics. Analyzing these different datasets helps businesses understand their performance, identify operational challenges, and make better decisions.

In this project, I developed a multi-page Power BI dashboard using the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

The dashboard transforms raw e-commerce data into interactive visual reports, allowing users to explore key business metrics, monitor performance, and investigate trends across different dimensions.

**Project focus:** Data Analysis | Business Intelligence | E-Commerce Analytics

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Understand freight costs and delivery performance.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns and repeat-customer behavior.
- Evaluate product and seller performance.
- Explore monthly sales trends and business growth.
- Present meaningful business information through interactive dashboards.

## 📂 Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

**Source:** [Kaggle – Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

The dataset contains information about orders placed on the Olist marketplace in Brazil.

The project uses business-related datasets covering:

- Orders
- Customers
- Order Items
- Products
- Sellers
- Payments
- Reviews
- Product Category Translation
- Geolocation

These datasets provide different perspectives on e-commerce operations, customer activity, sales transactions, and logistics.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Analysis

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall e-commerce performance.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Total Customers
- Total Items Sold

**Analysis:**
- Monthly sales trends
- Monthly order trends
- Sales by product category
- Orders by category
- Order status distribution
- Payment method distribution

### 2. Sales & Freight Analysis

Focuses on the relationship between sales performance, shipping costs, and delivery operations.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Sales
- Freight Percentage of Sales
- Average Order Value
- Average Freight per Order
- Total Freight

**Analysis:**
- Sales versus freight trends
- Freight by state
- Freight by product category
- Average delivery time trends
- Freight by order value range

### 3. Customer 360°

Explores customer behavior, purchasing frequency, and customer value.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Customers
- Repeat Customers
- Orders per Customer
- Customer Lifetime Value
- Repeat Customer Rate

**Analysis:**
- Customer segmentation
- Customer purchase frequency
- Customer value groups
- Customer distribution by state
- New customer trends

### 4. Product & Seller Analysis

Examines product category performance, product pricing, and seller distribution.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Products
- Total Sellers
- Total Product Sales
- Average Product Price
- Average Freight per Item

**Analysis:**
- Product sales by category
- Average product price by category
- Top sellers by product sales
- Seller distribution by state
- Monthly product sales trends
- Product price distribution

### 5. Insights & Forecast

Provides a consolidated view of business trends and additional analytical observations.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Sales
- Sales Growth Percentage
- Average Order Value
- Average Product Price
- Forecast Sales

**Analysis:**
- Monthly sales trends
- Monthly sales growth
- Product category performance
- Repeat customer alert
- Forecast-related analysis

This dashboard is designed to help answer questions such as:

1. How are sales and orders changing over time?
2. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
3. Which states contribute most to freight costs?
4. What percentage of sales is spent on freight?
5. How frequently do customers place repeat orders?
6. How is customer value distributed?
7. Which sellers contribute most to product sales?
8. How do product prices vary across categories?
9. What patterns can be observed in monthly sales performance?
10. What business trends can support better planning?
